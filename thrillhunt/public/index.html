Safety first

Every listing shows access rules, known hazards and operating status. Where we don't know, we say so instead of guessing.

18+ ONLY

THRILLHUNT connects adults with haunted attractions, outdoor adventures, camping destinations and other adrenaline-focused experiences. Some activities and locations involve inherent risks. You're responsible for deciding whether an activity is right for you and for following all applicable laws, property rules, posted warnings and safety requirements.

What THRILLHUNT will never do

Help anyone trespass, enter closed or restricted property, bypass security, or ignore posted warnings. Restricted places are listed only so people stop asking how to get in — and we point them at the legal, guided alternative instead.
